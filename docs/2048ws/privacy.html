2048WS Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 11, 2026 · App: 2048WS (current iPhone release; additional platforms in development)

2048WS is a single-player number puzzle designed without account registration or an advertising-supported business model.

Information used by the app

2048WS saves game-related information on your device, including the current board, best score, remaining daily free starts, and your sound or haptic preferences. This allows the app to resume your game, apply the daily limit, and remember your settings.

The current app does not ask you to create an account or provide your name, address or contact details to play. 2048WS does not contain advertising or third-party behavioral analytics SDKs, and we do not operate a gameplay-data backend for this app.

In-app purchases

When you choose the optional lifetime unlock, Apple processes your purchase via the App Store. The app uses Apple StoreKit to check and restore purchase entitlements. We do not directly process payments or receive your credit card number. Apple's processing of purchase and account data is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Nearby devices and local network access

Upcoming versions of 2048WS include optional nearby-device controls. When this feature is present and used, the app may request local network access to discover compatible iPad or Mac instances through Apple's Bonjour and MultipeerConnectivity technologies. The app may display a locally generated peer name, connection status and send gameplay direction commands within the local session. Those commands are used to control the chosen device's game board; they do not synchronize personal accounts or send your game board to a 2048WS-operated cloud server.

Nearby device discovery requires permission from the operating system. Use this feature on a trusted local network; nearby peer names and status may be visible to other compatible devices on that network. This feature is in development and not a feature of the initial iPhone-only App Store release.

Apple Watch connectivity

Future compatible Apple Watch features may exchange game controls, connection state and purchase-entitlement status between a paired iPhone and Apple Watch through Apple's device communication services. These features will require a compatible release and device; they are not part of the initial iPhone-only release.

Retention and your choices

On-device gameplay data and preferences remain available until removed via app or device controls. Deleting the app can remove locally stored progress. Device backups or Apple's services may retain or restore data subject to your device and Apple Account settings.

You can disable music, sound and haptic feedback in Settings. The optional in-app purchase is not necessary to use the free game allowance.

Support requests and other services

If you voluntarily send a support message through GitHub Issues, your message may be public. Please do not include passwords, purchase receipts with personal details, or other sensitive information. GitHub processes information under its privacy statement.

This informational website is hosted on GitHub Pages and contains no advertising or third-party analytics code added by 2048WS. The hosting service may process technical access logs according to its policies.

Children's privacy

2048WS does not ask players for personal details as a condition of play. Parents or guardians with questions can use the support page. Please do not post children's personal information in public support issues.

Policy changes

We may update this policy as app features and data practices change. Updates will be posted here with a revised effective date.

Contact

For privacy questions, email whitesharkteam@163.com or visit 2048WS Support. Please avoid publishing personal information in public support threads.
