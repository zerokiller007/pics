Getting started

How to play

  1. Open 2048WS on your iPhone. The current game board appears immediately.
  2. Swipe up, down, left, or right to slide all tiles.
  3. When two tiles of the same number collide, they merge into one larger tile.
  4. Try to create the 2048 tile, then keep going for a higher score.

Use Undo to reverse a move when available and Reset to start a new game. Your current board is automatically saved for you to resume.

Frequently asked questions

Why am I limited to five free new games per day?

2048WS lets you start up to five new games per local calendar day for free. Continuing an existing game does not use another free start. You can remove the limit with the optional one-time unlock.

Can I keep playing when I have zero free starts left?

Yes. You can continue the current board even when the daily allowance is exhausted. The limit applies to starting a new board.

How do I buy unlimited new games?

Open the Permanent Unlock / Unlimited Play purchase sheet from the game screen. If the product is available, follow the Apple in-app purchase prompts. The final price is displayed by Apple's App Store in your local currency.

How do I restore my lifetime unlock?

Open the in-app unlock sheet and tap Restore Purchases. Make sure you use the same Apple Account that originally purchased the unlock, and allow time for the App Store to respond. If it still does not work, check your purchase history and contact support.

Is there a subscription or advertising?

No. The unlock is a one-time, non-consumable in-app purchase. 2048WS does not include advertisements or recurring subscriptions.

How can I change music, sounds, and vibration?

Tap Settings. You can toggle background music and sound effects, change their volume, and enable or disable haptic feedback.

Will my board survive closing the app?

Your current game and settings are saved on the device. Reopen the app to resume. Deleting the app or clearing its data may remove locally stored game progress.

Why does an unlock say the product is unavailable?

Purchases depend on App Store availability and on the product's configuration. Confirm your device is online, restart the app, and try again later. During TestFlight testing, Apple uses its sandbox purchase environment.

Does 2048WS currently include Apple Watch, iPad or Mac versions?

The initial App Store release is for iPhone. Apple Watch gameplay, iPad two-player play and iPhone remote control of compatible iPad/Mac devices are being tested for future releases. These capabilities are not yet available in the initial release. Check the actual App Store version notes for availability.

How does nearby iPad/Mac remote control work when available?

The supported devices must run a compatible version of 2048WS on the same trusted local network, with local network permission granted. Open the single-player board on the target device and select it from the iPhone remote screen. Commands move tiles on the chosen device; game progress is not mirrored or uploaded. This feature is part of a future release, not the initial iPhone version.

What if a nearby device does not appear?

Confirm both devices have a compatible app version, are on the same trusted network, and have granted local network access. Keep the target app open and use the refresh control. If a device remains unavailable, send the device models, system versions and approximate steps to support without sharing private network details.

Platform availability

Current release: iPhone standalone 2048. In development: Apple Watch play, iPad solo/two-player play, and nearby remote control for compatible iPad and Apple silicon Mac. Do not rely on these future features until your installed App Store version specifically includes them.

Contact support

Privacy note: GitHub issues are public. Do not post passwords, purchase receipts containing personal data, contact details, or Apple Account credentials.

Billing and refunds

In-app purchases are processed by Apple. For charges, refunds, and billing history, visit Apple's Report a Problem website. For problems unlocking features after payment, use the Restore Purchases option first.
